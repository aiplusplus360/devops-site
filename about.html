Our approach

Boring infrastructure, shipped deliberately

Good DevOps work is invisible. If we do our job well, your deploys become uneventful, your on-call pages get quiet, and nobody outside the team notices anything changed at all.

You keep the keys

Repositories, cloud accounts and billing stay in your name from day one. We work inside your estate, never beside it.

Small, reviewable changes

Every change lands as a pull request with a rationale attached. Your team reviews it, learns from it, and can revert it.

Documented, then handed over

Runbooks, diagrams and a recorded walkthrough come standard. Nobody should need us to explain your own platform.

The process

Four steps from audit to autonomy

    What we bring

    Senior engineers, not a body shop

    You work directly with the people writing the automation. No account managers relaying messages, no junior hand-offs after the sales call.

    • Named engineer assigned to your engagement
    • Shared Slack or Teams channel for the duration
    • Weekly written progress updates with blockers surfaced early
    • Cost, reliability and security reviewed at every stage
    • Exit plan documented from the start — no lock-in by design
